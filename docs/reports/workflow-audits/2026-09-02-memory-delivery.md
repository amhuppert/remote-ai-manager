## Verdict

The run delivered the whole spec — 12/12 contexts, 31/31 claimed criteria GO, one clean lane merge, no scratch debris — and its final-verification context produced unusually strong evidence: a genuinely live two-backend propagation proof (Codex answered from a hook with zero tool calls), a byte-identical Index Preview, 10/10 corpus recall twice, and a comparative evaluation whose headline finding (recency-ordered eviction under budget pressure) is a real product insight. Total spend is **≈ $310 (a floor)** — implementer ≈ $261 after adding two conversations the extractor omitted, plus ≈ $49 estimated validator usage — over 22 hours of wall clock, of which ≈ 17h was implementer turns. Validators rejected 10 of 12 contexts at least once (39 iterations for 12 contexts), and the deep-reads show those NO-GOs were overwhelmingly real production defects; the avoidable spend (roughly $35–45) came from three engine behaviors — rotation limits enforced only at task completion, validators reporting one gap per round, and operator pause/resume resetting started contexts — plus two criteria whose wording invited unbounded proof. The single highest-leverage change is **enforcing the rotation limit mid-iteration** (both 391k-token peaks and the $4–5 rotation ceremonies follow from it); the single highest-leverage planning change is **bounding criteria to what the embedder can prove** (the backend-neutralization spiral).

## Friction (ranked by quality impact)

1. **A criterion demanded an unprovable proof** (`native-disabled-where-supported`: "disabled in every CC-launched environment, proven by a contract test") — evidence: three consecutive NO-GOs at 14:47, 15:18, 15:44Z each naming one deeper SDK policy layer (managed policy → `policyHelper` → `forceRemoteSettingsRefresh`); all three sit in the one installed `sdk.d.ts` (lines 2355/2642/6115) and the implementer had layer 1 in context at 14:04Z (T1[459–462]) and layer 2 at 14:48Z (T2[30–31]); its own one-command scanner at T2[1049] enumerated all three. Root cause: the SDK itself says `resolveSettings` "reports the raw settings cascade, not a security decision" — no pre-launch check can prove remote-mutable policy, so only refusal closes it. Shipped result: fail-closed launch refusals on managed override, declared policy helper, forced remote refresh, or unreadable cascade, plus a bump-time ratchet the implementer itself flagged as brittle (T2[1187]). Cost ≈ $8.1 for iterations 3–4 (T2 result entries) and a product behavior nobody asked for. Fix: **[planning-skill]** provider-setting criteria must be bounded to what the embedder controls ("CC sets the disable at the highest layer the SDK exposes; the descriptor enumerates higher-precedence sources once, up front; disclosure renders the residual"); **[product]** decide whether the fail-closed guard should ship as-is.
2. **Validators report the nearest gap per round, not the class** — evidence: backend-neutralization prompts at T2[1/674/954] each list exactly one issue; cli round 1 listed 7 gaps but round 2 found a sibling of one of them (not-found refusal echoing a UUID). Fix: **[engine]** verdict instructions should ask for the full discovered class of a finding ("enumerate sibling instances of the same kind before returning"); aligns with existing proposal 2.2 (adversarial verification of NO-GO findings).
3. **Rotation limit enforced only at task completion** — evidence: `decisions.jsonl` `rotation.scheduled` at 08:08:33Z with `contextTokens: 391298` vs limit 250,000 (policy-cascade iteration 1 ran ≈40 calls above the limit; block 3 peaked 382,496 and hit the hard `CONTEXT LIMIT` stop at [2120]); cli iteration 3 ran at ≈320k for entries 1221–1590 (114k over) and rotated with a truncated handoff. Consequences measured by the deep-reads: rotation ceremony ≈ $4.4 per rotation (cli), 28% of the post-rotation block spent re-reading 12 already-read files (policy-cascade B1), retained xhigh thinking = 21.5% of iteration-1 growth and 35.8% of block-3 growth. Fix: **[engine]** force a turn boundary at the threshold (the engine already computes per-message usage and owns the stop hook); aligns with proposal 3.4 (context-window budget management) and 6.1 (token budgets).
4. **Operator pause/resume reset started contexts** — evidence: `lifecycle.jsonl` `execution.paused {actor: operator}` at 02:51:06Z and 04:50:23Z with **no** `graph-workflow-live-edit-applied` event in either window (DB); each `execution.resumed` carries `resetContextIds` (`memory-session-lifecycle`, `memory-recall-ranking`); `iterations.jsonl` shows the pre-pause iterations (02:29→02:51 = 22m; 04:33→04:50 = 17m) abandoned and restarted in new conversations; the abandoned conversations `aa9cb5cc`/`e40bce32` have NULL cost and turns in `conversations`. Net: ≈ 38.5m idle + ≈ 39m of discarded, unpriced agent work. The operator's reason is not recorded. Fix: **[engine]** a pause on a started context must not discard its in-flight iteration on resume (or the UI must say it will); price and report reset conversations. Aligns with proposal 3.3 (mid-flight revision + resume with prefix preservation).
5. **Criteria wording gaps in `memory-cli` bought two rounds** — evidence: 5 of the 7 round-1 gaps were readable off the criteria (deep-read Q1: `--json` identity for recall/index named in criterion 3 but tested only for get/list at t1[195]; "active-only" tested by a test literally named for it that never tried a proposed note; byte-exactness codified `${text}\n`); the whole round 2 came from one rationalization at t1[249] ("a not_found refusal that echoes the caller's handle doesn't violate the invariant"). Fix: **[planning-skill]** criteria spanning surfaces × verbs ship as an enumerated matrix each cell mapped to a named test before `task complete`; invariants on "text output/refusals" must state whether a caller-supplied ID echoed back counts. Aligns with proposal 1.6 (shared rubric).
6. **Shared documents re-materialized over post-upsert edits** — evidence: policy-cascade [1754–1847] (doc patch 0 matches after the engine rewrote the registered doc; edits lost twice), backend-neutralization T2[815–853] (iteration-2 handoff edit lost, rewrite). ## Recommendations

1. **[planning-skill]** Add a criterion lint/guidance: an acceptance criterion may not require proof over state the embedder does not control (provider-side or remote-mutable settings); phrase it as "sets the strongest available control, enumerates the residual sources once, discloses the residual". Source: friction 1.
2. **[planning-skill]** Criteria that name several surfaces × verbs are authored as an enumerated matrix; invariants about text output/refusals define caller-echo explicitly; an implementer-discovered residual against its own criteria blocks `task complete`. Source: frictions 5, 10. Aligns with proposal 1.6.
3. **[engine]** Enforce `contextLimitTokens` mid-iteration with a forced turn boundary that sheds retained thinking; include rotated-out and continuation conversations in cost/turn accounting. Source: frictions 3, 8. Aligns with proposals 3.4 and 6.1.
4. **[engine]** Validator prompt: report the full class of a finding per round; consider the adversarial-verification pass (proposal 2.2) for any context on its third NO-GO of the same criterion.
5. **[engine]** Pause/resume must preserve a started context's in-flight iteration (or warn); reset conversations must be priced. Source: friction 4. Aligns with proposal 3.3.
6. **[engine]** Shared-document materialization must not overwrite post-upsert edits; relaunching a killed background validation must surface the surviving server-side verdict. Source: frictions 6, 9. Aligns with proposal 3.5.
7. **[config/guidance]** On whole-feature lane branches, replace repeated changed-scope test runs with one `--scope full` at context end; teach the planning skill that changed-scope is a merge-gate concept, not an in-loop one. Source: friction 7.
8. **[audit tooling]** Extractor: include rotated conversations, count continuation blocks, treat `tool_progress` heartbeats as activity in the hung-turn heuristic, classify answered questions, and count operator pauses as human wait. Source: friction 8.
9. **[product follow-ups]** From the evaluation: actionable omission line; auto-tail aging/cap; `mark-reviewed` semantics; the SSE double-emit bug; decide the fail-closed launch-guard policy. Source: outcome quality.
10. **[template/definition]** Keep: single ordered lane for shared-surface features, `allowAgentTaskAdd` on the final live-verification context, the corpus fixture as an in-repo evaluation substrate, and per-context model upgrades on the hardest contexts (four ran on Fable at xhigh; this audit cannot isolate their effect from criteria difficulty).
