01 · Executive read

No universal winner.

The market has separated into four useful positions. Fable retains a narrow lead on the broad Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index; Sol leads coding-agent and long-horizon professional work; Terra is the strongest price/performance compromise; Luna owns high-volume economics.

04 · Reasoning effort

Effort is a product dial.

Higher effort increases the model’s willingness to spend tokens, call tools, explore alternatives, and verify work. It is not a strict token budget. The economically correct setting is the lowest level that holds quality on your own evaluation set.

07 · Method & sources

Read the fine print.

Benchmark tables are snapshots, not laws of nature. Harness, effort, tool access, latency tier, safety configuration, and scoring method can move results materially.

Comparison rules

  • Official provider list prices are used, before batch or volume discounts.
  • Capability values come from the July 9 OpenAI comparison table; independent indices remain attributed to Artificial Analysis.
  • Equal-token task cost is arithmetic, not a claim that models consume equal tokens to complete work.
  • Reasoning effort changes output-token consumption and tool behavior; only measured workloads reveal true cost per successful task.

Known caveats

  • OpenAI publishes more complete cross-model tables for GPT‑5.6 than Anthropic does for competing GPT‑5.6 models.
  • Some competitor results are reproduced by providers rather than run by a neutral lab.
  • Safety fallbacks can change Fable’s behavior and effective model on cyber, biology, chemistry, and frontier-AI requests.
  • Speed evidence mixes token throughput with end-to-end task time; they are intentionally not collapsed into one score.
