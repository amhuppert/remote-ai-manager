Design narrative

The human reviews, comments, approves, signs off, waives, and configures gates — never edits content. Comments are how change requests reach the agent; every content change is a new draft revision produced by the agent.

Approvals are per-element records; the revision freezes only through an explicit sign-off. After an amendment, approvals carry forward on unchanged elements, flip to stale on modified ones, and close on removed ones.

native SDD — design prototype ·

Sources: docs/design/native-sdd/01-product-design.md (rev 5) · cc-design-system SKILL.md · src/components/ui primitives · tickets + workflow surfaces
Assumptions
  • Demo data dogfoods the native-sdd spec itself: R1–R12 (R9 removed in rev 4), D1–D4, T1–T13, Q1–Q2, A1–A3 — content derived from the product doc.
  • Updated to product design rev 5: composite phase projection, explicit review actions + three sign-off preconditions, first-class questions/assumptions, proof verdicts distinct from evidence, human-only waivers with staleness, gate admissions vs approvals, provenance-locked definition regions, deterministic scope validation, chip staleness.
  • All list rows open the native-sdd detail; the prototype models one spec deeply rather than seven shallowly.
  • Spec list is a navigation peer of Tickets: a Specs topbar destination, project-scoped via the breadcrumb.
  • Phase→accent mapping: Draft neutral · In review amber · Approved / Delivered green · Executing cyan · Abandoned muted. Amber is reserved for "a human is awaited" per the system.
  • Sign-off is modeled as a modal act with an explicit acknowledgment — heavier than element approvals, per B7.
How to review
  • The dashed PROTOTYPE bar (bottom right) switches screens (keys 1–9, 0 for this page; T Traceability · H History · N Entry points), scenario, and the design-notes layer.
  • Scenario changes the world state: In review (default) · Draft · Just created · Executing · Delivered · Exploratory · Abandoned · Empty.
  • Approvals, bulk actions, gate dials, filters, and sign-off are live — click through the review flow end to end.
  • Notes ON shows per-screen interaction annotations in a right-side panel.
